## Full description (both stores)

Read the complete King James Version Bible anywhere — no internet connection
required. The full text of all 66 books ships inside the app.

FULLY OFFLINE
Every verse is on your device from the moment you install. Airplane mode,
remote areas, no data plan — the Bible is always there.

LISTEN TO ANY CHAPTER
Built-in audio playback reads any chapter aloud with adjustable speed and
verse-by-verse highlighting, and keeps playing with the screen off.

STUDY TOOLS
- Highlight verses in multiple colors
- Attach personal notes to any verse
- Bookmark passages and jump back instantly
- Cross-references for deeper study
- Red-letter edition: words of Christ highlighted

FAST SEARCH
Full-text search across the whole Bible, or scoped to the Old or New
Testament, with search history.

READING PLANS
Built-in plans (Proverbs, Psalms, Gospel of John, Epistles) with per-day
progress tracking, plus a daily Verse of the Day.

MADE FOR READING
Choose from ten carefully selected reading typefaces (including
dyslexia-friendly OpenDyslexic and Atkinson Hyperlegible), adjust text size,
and pick light, dark, or colored themes.

MORE TRANSLATIONS
Optionally download additional public-domain translations — the only feature
that uses the network, and only when you ask for it.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Your highlights, notes, and reading
progress stay on your device.

## Privacy declarations

The app collects **no user data**. All notes, highlights, bookmarks, and
reading progress are stored locally (Hive) and never leave the device.

- Network use: a single outbound HTTPS endpoint, `api.getbible.net`, used
  only when the user explicitly downloads an additional translation. No
  analytics, no ads SDKs, no crash reporting SDK (as of this version).
- Play Data Safety form: "No data collected", "No data shared". Declare
  that data is not encrypted in transit N/A (nothing is transmitted except
  the public translation download over HTTPS).
- App Store privacy ("nutrition label"): Data Not Collected.
- iOS `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is already set to `false` (HTTPS
  only — exempt).
